## Globe geography

The simplified world boundaries in `lib/globe-coastlines.json` are derived from the GeoJSON in Akshun Chauhan’s [Portfolio-](https://github.com/AkshunChauhan/Portfolio-/blob/main/data/globe.json) reference portfolio. The canvas projection, interaction, and rendering are implemented locally without Three.js.
