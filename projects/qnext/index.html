The question

Presentations can make it difficult for everyone in a room to participate. Qnext connects presenters and participants through a shared audience engagement platform.

The approach

Built a Next.js and TypeScript platform with Firebase and Google Genkit. QR-based entry connects the audience to live presentations, while multi-tenant data models and analytics dashboards track participation, content access, and engagement.

The result

A public web platform bringing audience access, live interaction, and presentation organization into one product. The repository and project demo show how the platform connects presenters with participants.
